Our Process

The 3-Phase Exit Prep Roadmap

A structured 12–36 month operational clean-up path to prepare your business for sale.

Phase 01

Systems & Billing Cleanup

We integrate automated payment routes and merchant processing (via RooferLedger models) to replace manual administrative flows. This increases cash velocity, reduces outstanding receivables (A/R), and lowers transaction fees to immediately increase net profitability.

Phase 02

Overhead & Margin Audit

We deploy CostUtilityHub to audit historical utility tariffs, commercial telecommunication billing, and local tax schedules. Recouping billing overcharges creates clean, retroactive EBITDA adjustments that lift cash flow sheets.

Phase 03

Standardization & Exit Ready

We package your operational guides, automate system dependencies to eliminate founder-key-man risk, and compile three years of clean, ledger-backed EBITDA financials. This leaves buyers with a turn-key business that commands premium multiples.
